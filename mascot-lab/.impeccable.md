## Design Context

### Users

Project owner comparing tiny mascot candidates locally before choosing a dashboard placement. Task: select characters, compare animated state/personality, inspect legibility at small sizes, and test a dashboard-adjacent preview. This bench is not an emergency response UI.

### Brand Personality

User reference: Grok-bot-like mini agents, pixelated, several distinct mascots. Original, friendly, quietly capable. Small expressive machine faces, not copied branding or an operational authority.

### Aesthetic Direction

Premium neutral gallery with tiny 32x32 retro sprites. Geist Sans, optional mono metadata, blue #006bff only for interaction, no orange and no emoji. Dark default with a real light comparison. Quiet hairlines and clear typography borrow existing Team/FCC and design-demos control language; mascot silhouettes supply personality.

### Design Principles

1. Characters remain legible and distinct at dashboard scale, not just in a hero.
2. Show an expressive family of originals without copying Grok/xAI assets.
3. Make local visual-demo status unmistakable; no fictional operational success claims.
4. Every visible control works through mouse and keyboard. Respect reduced motion and explicit pause.
5. Standalone bench only. Dashboard owner chooses whether and where to integrate.
